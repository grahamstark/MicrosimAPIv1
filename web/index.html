The Marginal Effective Tax Rate (METR) is the amount of the next £1 you earn that you get to keep, once direct taxes have been paid and any income-related benefits you receive have been reduced.

The headline poverty rate is the percentage of the Scottish population that live in households with income below 60% of the median. The Child Poverty rate is the percentage of children in such households.
